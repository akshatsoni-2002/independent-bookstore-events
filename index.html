PLAIN-LANGUAGE POLICY

Privacy, with
no fine print surprises.

This prototype runs in your browser. The demo does not send event data to a server.

Data stored in this demo

Event records, saved-event preferences, theme preference, and demo staff accounts may be stored in browser storage on the device where you use the site.

What this means

Data is not shared across devices or users. Anyone with access to the same browser may be able to inspect or modify local demo data. Do not enter real staff passwords, private customer details, or confidential business information.

Production deployment

A live commercial service needs a reviewed privacy policy, secure server-side authentication, appropriate access controls, a defined retention policy, and a secure database.
